# Callya community code of conduct

We want participation in Callya to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information, especially recordings, transcripts, credentials,
  and other call data.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and useful.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, reject participation, or impose a
temporary or permanent ban when necessary. To report conduct privately, use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/kotlyar/ai-call-assistant/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[conduct]`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially
and fairly.

This code applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing the Callya project.
